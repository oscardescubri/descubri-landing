# descubri-landing

descubri.app: the Descubri company page (`index.html`).

- The photo app's original pages are archived in `archive/photo-app/` (not indexed); `_redirects` sends the old `/about` and `/shot-recipes` there.
- `privacy.html` and `terms.html` are the photo app's legal pages, kept at `/privacy` and `/terms` because the app store listings link to them.
- `.well-known/` holds the photo app's iOS and Android deep-link files. Keep them.
- Seasonal Contracts' own terms and privacy live in that app (`/terms`, `/privacy` on its address).

Deployed to Cloudflare Pages (project `descubri-landing`).
